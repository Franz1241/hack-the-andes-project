## Rules

- Talk to people outside the team: carriers, logistics staff and agroexport operations.
- Log role and company type only. No names, phone numbers or emails, because the repo will be public.
- Keep evidence we can show judges: a screenshot, a message, or a quote they agreed to share.

## Interview script (10 minutes)

1. How do you plan a trip on a road like the Carretera Central today?
2. When did a huaico, landslide or closure last hit one of your trips? What did it cost in time, cargo or money?
3. Where do you check road and weather status now?
4. After seeing ILLAPA: would this recommendation change what you do? What is missing?
5. Would you try it on your next trip? Can we follow up?
